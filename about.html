Our Story

About the Ethiopian Cadet Association

A professional body serving Ethiopian maritime professionals since our founding, building bridges between training and career success.

Our Mission

Empowering Ethiopia's Maritime Workforce

The Ethiopian Cadet Association (ECA) was established to serve as the professional home for Ethiopian marine engineering cadets and officers. We are dedicated to advancing the maritime careers of our members through education, advocacy, networking, and financial support.

Ethiopia has a rich connection to the sea — and we are committed to ensuring that Ethiopian maritime professionals are equipped, certified, and recognized on the global stage. From the Red Sea coast to shipping lanes worldwide, ECA members represent the best of Ethiopian seafaring excellence.

🎯 Mission: Develop world-class Ethiopian maritime professionals
👁 Vision: A globally respected Ethiopian maritime community
⚓ Values: Excellence, Unity, Integrity, Innovation
📅 Founded: Over 10 years of service
What We Do

Our Core Activities

🎓

Training & Education

We guide cadets through pre-sea and post-sea training programs, connecting them with accredited institutions and coaching them through certification processes.

🤝

Professional Networking

We connect Ethiopian mariners across the globe through events, forums, and our online member community — building lifelong professional relationships.

📢

Advocacy

We represent the interests of Ethiopian maritime professionals in dealings with shipping companies, maritime authorities, and international organizations.

💰

Financial Support

Through scholarships, grants, and partnerships, we help qualified cadets fund their training and certification without financial barriers.

📰

Information & Resources

We provide up-to-date maritime news, career guidance, regulatory updates, and industry reports to keep members informed.

🌍

International Connections

ECA maintains relationships with IMO, international shipping associations, and maritime academies worldwide to create opportunities for our members.
